Buff is a nail salon on Le Pollet in St Peter Port. We focus on nails only, with a wide choice of colours and designs painted by hand.

What we do

Pick a treatment to see what’s included.

Not sure what to go for? Call the salon on 01481 713629 and talk it through.

Manicures

Nails shaped and tidied, then finished in a colour you choose from our shelf. Keep it glossy and simple, or add glitter to a nail or two.

What’s included

  • Shaping and filing
  • Cuticle tidy
  • Your choice of colour
  • Glitter accents if you’d like them
Book a manicure

French tips

A sheer, natural base with a clean line at the tip. Have it classic white, or in any colour, from soft pastels to bright neon.

What’s included

  • Shaping: square, round or almond
  • Sheer natural base
  • White or coloured tips
  • Even, crisp smile lines
Book French tips

Nail art

Designs painted by hand, from a single fine line to swirls edged in glitter. Bring a picture or an idea, or we can suggest something.

What’s included

  • Fine lines and abstract shapes
  • Waves, swirls and colour blocks
  • Glitter detailing
  • One feature nail or all ten
